Our story

A little home bakery, baked with a lot of heart

Kaaf sy Cake began with a simple idea: celebrations deserve cakes made the way home baking should be. Fresh, thoughtful and personal.

Every cake is prepared to order and never mass-produced, so what you receive is made specifically for your moment. We bake in small batches in our home kitchen in Lahore, and we finish every cake by hand.

We work closely with you on flavor, design and size to bring your idea to life, from everyday treats to custom celebration cakes. That is why we built the Cake Studio: so you can show us what you imagine, and we can talk it through together before anything goes in the oven.
